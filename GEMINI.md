# Project Overview: 2026 UI/UX Design Course (Figma × AI)

This project contains the complete curriculum, lesson plans, and instructional materials for a **33-hour UI/UX Design Course**. The course is designed for beginners and focuses on mastering **Figma** while integrating **AI tools** (like ChatGPT and Claude) into the modern design workflow.

## Curriculum Structure

The course is divided into **11 Units**, each lasting **3 hours**. Every unit follows a consistent **"1-1-1 Model"**:
1.  **1 Hour Lecture & Demo:** Theoretical concepts combined with live Figma demonstrations.
2.  **1 Hour Hands-on Practice:** In-class exercises based on the lecture topics.
3.  **1 Hour Discussion & Review:** Peer review and instructor feedback on the practical work.

## Key Files & Directories

- **`class.md`**: The master curriculum document. It contains the high-level course objectives, the 11-unit schedule, and detailed descriptions of the 12 major exercises.
- **`README.md`**: Brief project overview and reference links for Figma best practices.
- **`Unit_XX/`**: Individual directories for each course unit (Unit_01 to Unit_11).
- **`Unit_XX/Unit_XX.md`**: Detailed lesson plans for each unit, including specific time allocations and discussion points.

## Teaching Conventions

- **Tooling**: Figma is the primary design tool. AI tools are used for requirement analysis, persona generation, copy writing, and brainstorming.
- **Deliverables**: Most exercises result in a Figma file or a PDF/Presentation report.
- **Documentation**: All lesson plans and curriculum updates are maintained in Markdown format within the respective unit folders.
- **Feedback Loop**: The 3rd hour of every unit is dedicated to discussion, ensuring immediate feedback on the day's practical work.

## Usage

When interacting with this project, refer to `class.md` for the overall roadmap and individual `Unit_XX.md` files for granular lesson details. AI should be treated as a "design partner" throughout the curriculum, assisting with content and analysis rather than just generating final visuals.
